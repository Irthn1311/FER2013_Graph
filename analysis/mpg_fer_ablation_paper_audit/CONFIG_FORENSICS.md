# Forensic Configuration Audit: Cumulative A4/A5 vs Older Table VI Ablations

## Executive Summary of Findings

1. **Discrepancy Root Cause:** The term `FIXED_POOL` has two entirely distinct semantic meanings in this repository:
   - **In Table VI (Old FIXED_POOL):** Refers to **Fixed Spatial Pooling** (from 2304 pixels to 49 motif nodes, bypassing learned prototypes/SMC), but retains **learnable attention pooling** in the motif readout. It achieved **70.02% TTA**.
   - **In Cumulative Ladder (Cumulative A5):** Refers to **Fixed Motif Readout Pooling** (uniform mean over 49 occurrences in motif readout), but retains the **learned multi-scale Spatial Motif Composer** and **Dynamic Top-K**. It achieved **64.59% TTA**.
   - Consequently, Cumulative A5 and Old FIXED_POOL are **completely different model architectures** that happen to share a similar label. They cannot and should not match.

2. **P4 Semantic Equivalence:** The older **DENSE_MOTIF** ablation from Table VI is an **exact 100% semantic and structural match** for the target cumulative candidate **P4** (+ Learnable Motif Readout with dense relations).
   - It possesses full multi-scale composition, geometry bias, dense 5-layer Motif GNN, and learnable attention readout.
   - It achieves **70.33% TTA**, creating a natural, positive step between P3 (65.73%) and Full Model P5 (70.66%).
